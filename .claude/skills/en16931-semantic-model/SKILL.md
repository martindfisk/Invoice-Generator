# EN 16931 — European core invoice

Standard: EN 16931-1:2017 (+A1:2019) semantic model; EN 16931-1:2026 revision approved Feb 2026 (details unconfirmed — cite 2017 unless verified). Syntax bindings: UBL 2.1 (CEN/TS 16931-3-2), UN/CEFACT CII D16B (16931-3-3). Legal anchor: Directive 2014/55/EU (public entities must receive/process compliant e-invoices). National CIUS/extensions: XRechnung (DE), Peppol BIS 3.0, EXTENDED-CTC-FR; FatturaPA is a national schema, not an EN 16931 syntax.

## Calculation rules the model enforces (`model-rules.ts`)
BR-CO-10 BT-106 = Σ BT-131 · BR-CO-11/12 BT-107/108 = Σ document allowances/charges · BR-CO-13 BT-109 = BT-106 − BT-107 + BT-108 · BR-CO-14 BT-110 = Σ BT-117 · BR-CO-15 BT-112 = BT-109 + BT-110 · BR-CO-16 BT-115 = BT-112 − BT-113 + BT-114 · BR-CO-17 BT-117 = BT-116 × (BT-119 ÷ 100), rounded to 2 decimals · BR-CO-18 at least one BG-23 · BR-S-08 per standard rate: BT-116 = Σ BT-131 (S, rate) + allowances − charges · BR-CO-3/4 line VAT category and rate consistent with breakdown · BR-CO-9 VAT ids prefixed with ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 · BR-CO-25 due date or payment terms when BT-115 > 0 · BR-CO-26 seller needs BT-29, BT-30 or BT-31. Category rules BR-S/Z/E/AE/K/G/O-01..10 govern when rates and exemption reasons must or must not appear.
