2 · XML-only and Human-only

The segmented control is the only mode switch and is present in all three states. Mode is persisted per session; below 1280 px Split stays selectable but renders stacked. Selection survives a mode change — here the selection came from the XML side (source: "xml") and the Human view would scroll its VAT-breakdown row into view when Split or Human is chosen.

3 · Findings panel

Preset “Intentionally broken”, so all four severities are present at once. The list is a single-select listbox with a roving tabindex: ↑/↓ move the active option and the selection, which republishes {path, field, source: "finding"} to both panes. The selected finding — 00401 — drives the wavy error underline in the excerpt on the right; the other findings only leave gutter markers.
